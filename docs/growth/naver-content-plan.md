## Publishing Rule

- Do not auto-publish external posts in this PR.
- Do not create pages by changing only numbers.
- Create a new guide only after at least 28 days of repeated search demand and when existing pages cannot satisfy intent.
- Each topic needs a unique table, scenario, or interpretation and a natural calculator CTA.

## Format

- Short answer first.
- One compact table per post.
- Explain assumptions and basis date.
- Link to one primary calculator and at most two supporting calculators.
- Do not copy on-site body text verbatim.
